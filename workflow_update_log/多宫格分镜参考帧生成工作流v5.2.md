# 多宫格分镜参考帧生成工作流（v5.3：Concrete Anchor Prompt + Orientation Visibility Control + Position-Pose Separation）

## 0. 版本定位

本版本是在 v5.2 的基础上继续收紧 prompt 规则，重点解决四个实际问题：

1. **人物在场景中的位置不稳定**：不能只写“standing on the center of the platform / standing in the plaza center”，而应使用场景图中已经存在、视觉明确的参考锚点来定位，例如圆形纹理、台阶边缘、石柱、桌角、窗户、门框、光源、裂纹等。
2. **人物朝向与镜头关系不稳定**：写 prompt 前必须先判断“人物与目标物的关系”和“人物与镜头的关系”。如果人物背对镜头，就不能写 facial structure、expression、calm face 等正面/神态词，而应写 back-view details，例如 back of hairstyle、shoulder line、robe folds、waist belt、sleeves、boots。
3. **抽象词导致模型误解**：不要用 distance、closer、farther、partially visible、slightly、subtle 等抽象限制词来控制画面。必须改成“最终可见关系”，例如鞋尖与石柱底座外缘的关系、手指与道具接触点的关系、背后能看到哪些细节以及哪些脸部结构不能出现。
4. **位置控制与动作表现混淆**：当目标是改变人物最终位置时，必须用鞋子、脚尖、地面纹理、底座边缘等锚点锁定落点；允许出现 one-step forward stance / one foot forward 这类可接受姿势，但它只能作为姿态补充，不能替代最终位置锚点。

v5.3 仍然保留 v5 的核心要求：最终可复制 prompt 必须为英文一段式；每帧最多参考两张图；必须使用真实文件名；分镜需要同时满足 story continuity、camera variation、visible-content control、candidate selection。

---

## 1. 核心目标

用于指导大模型基于用户 idea 逐步生成 **N=4 / 9 / 16 / 25** 的多宫格分镜参考帧。

核心目标：

- 剧情连续：相邻帧在动作、视线、道具状态、场景交互上有明确承接；
- 镜头多样：通过景别、机位、主体比例、构图重点变化体现剧情推进；
- 角色一致：人物身份、发型、服装、配饰、体型保持稳定；
- 场景一致：光源、空间结构、关键参考物位置保持稳定；
- 位置稳定：人物位置必须用场景内可见锚点描述，而不是抽象方位词；
- 距离稳定：人物与目标物远近必须通过鞋子、脚尖、手、底座、地面纹理、同心圆等可见关系表达，而不是写 distance / closer / farther；
- 朝向稳定：先判断人物面对谁、背对谁，再决定描述正面细节还是背面细节；
- 可见性稳定：不要写“侧脸若隐若现”这类模糊限制，而要写镜头能看到哪些背面/侧面细节，以及哪些脸部结构不能出现；
- 输出稳定：最终 prompt 简洁、英文、一段式，可直接复制到 Qwen-Image-Edit 使用。

## 2. 总体流程

### Step 1：接收用户输入

用户通常输入：

- 一个简单 idea；
- 宫格数量 N，通常为 4 / 9 / 16 / 25；
- 可选：风格、人物数量、场景数量、关键道具、关键动作；
- 可选：每帧是否一次生成多张候选图。

### Step 2：整体策划

首次响应时，模型输出：

1. 视频概述；
2. 最少人物与场景数量；
3. 资源库构建 prompt；
4. N 帧分镜规划；
5. 只给出 `case_frame01.png` 的英文一段式 prompt。

不要一次性给出所有帧的详细 prompt。只给当前要生成的一帧。

### Step 3：逐帧生成与选择

用户上传当前帧结果后：

- 如果是单张图：先质检，合格再给下一帧 prompt；
- 如果是多张候选图：先执行 candidate selection，选出正式帧，再给下一帧 prompt；
- 如果当前帧不合格：给出重生成 prompt，不进入下一帧。

## 3. 命名规则

- 人物参考图：`case_char_01.png`，多人物则 `case_char_02.png`、`case_char_03.png`
- 场景参考图：`case_scene_01.png`，多场景则 `case_scene_02.png`
- 道具参考图：`case_prop_01.png`
- 正式分镜帧：`case_frame01.png`、`case_frame02.png`、……、`case_frame09.png`
- 同一帧候选图：`case_frame03_A.png`、`case_frame03_B.png`、`case_frame03_C.png`
- 选中候选后必须重命名为正式帧，例如 `case_frame03_B.png` → `case_frame03.png`

最终 prompt 中不能写 `the previous image`、`Image 0`、`reference image`、`this image`、`last frame`。必须写真实文件名。

## 4. 资源库构建规则

### 4.1 人物参考图

人物参考图用于锁定完整角色形象，而不是只锁定脸部。

应包含：

- 全身入镜；
- 正面或 3/4 正面视角；
- 发型、发饰、脸型、服装轮廓、腰带、袖口、靴子、配饰清楚；
- 纯色或简洁背景；
- 不要场景、复杂特效、额外人物。

示例：

```text
Generate case_char_01.png: a full-body character reference sheet of Wang Lin, a young male Chinese fantasy cultivator with black hair tied in a topknot, wearing a flowing blue robe with silver embroidery, belt, boots, and small accessories, standing naturally from head to toe on a plain light background, front three-quarter view, clear facial structure, outfit silhouette, sleeve details, waist belt, and boots, no scene, no magic effects, no extra characters, cinematic Chinese fantasy 3D animation style.
```

### 4.2 场景参考图

场景参考图不仅要锁定整体氛围，还要锁定后续定位可用的**视觉锚点**。

应包含：

- 空间结构；
- 关键地标或家具位置；
- 地面纹理、台阶、圆形纹路、门框、窗户、石柱、桌角等可定位元素；
- 光源方向；
- 最好不要人物。

示例：

```text
Generate case_scene_01.png: an empty ancient lava cave interior, clearly showing the spatial layout with a central circular stone platform, multi-ringed circular texture on the platform surface, surrounding molten lava flows, platform edge stones, rocky walls, cave opening, stalactites, and dim orange ambient lighting from the lava, no characters, no props, pure environment reference, cinematic Chinese fantasy 3D animation style.
```

### 4.3 道具参考图

若剧情依赖关键道具，则单独生成道具图。

道具参考图应锁定：外形、材质、大小、纹理、发光状态、朝向、纯色背景或简单台面。

## 5. 分镜规划规则

模型需要先规划完整 N 帧内容，但只输出当前帧详细 prompt。

每帧规划表必须包含：

| 字段 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 帧 | `case_frame0x.png` |
| 剧情节点 | 这一帧承担的剧情功能 |
| 镜头设计 | shot size + angle + viewpoint |
| 位置锚点 | 人物/道具相对哪个场景锚点定位 |
| 朝向关系 | 人物面对目标物还是背对目标物；人物面向镜头还是背对镜头 |
| 画面重点 | 当前镜头应该清楚看到什么 |
| 连续性变化 | 相比上一帧的动作、道具、场景状态变化 |

### 5.1 9 宫格推荐节奏

9 宫格推荐结构：

1. **Establishing wide shot**：建立人物与场景关系；
2. **Medium action shot**：人物进入动作状态；
3. **Close-up**：锁定人物注意或能量反应；
4. **Insert shot**：手部、道具、火焰、裂纹等关键细节；
5. **Back view / over-the-shoulder**：建立人物视线与目标物关系；
6. **Close-up contact shot**：触碰、拿取、施法等接触点；
7. **Medium low-angle shot**：反应或力量变化；
8. **Dynamic wide / low-angle shot**：高潮；
9. **Static wide / aftermath shot**：收束。

## 6. 场景锚点定位规则（Anchor-Based Positioning）

### 6.1 必须使用场景内可见锚点

当 prompt 描述人物或道具位置时，优先使用场景图中已经存在、视觉明确的锚点。

推荐写法：

```text
The figure stands at the center of the multi-ringed circular texture on the stone platform.
```

```text
The character stands just behind the front edge stones of the circular platform, aligned with the center ring pattern.
```

```text
The jade scroll lies on the empty wooden surface between the brush holder and the red seal, slightly above the front edge of the table.
```

不推荐写法：

```text
The character stands in the center of the platform.
```

```text
The object is on the right side of the table.
```

```text
The figure stands near the pillar.
```

原因：center / right / near 这类抽象方位词容易被模型误解；已有纹理、边缘、柱子、桌角、窗框、台阶等可见锚点更稳定。

### 6.2 场景参考图应主动生成锚点

如果后续分镜需要人物多次定位，场景参考图 prompt 中应提前加入可复用锚点，例如：

- `multi-ringed circular texture on the platform surface`
- `front edge stones of the platform`
- `single stone pillar centered in the plaza`
- `cobblestone ground cracks radiating around the pillar base`
- `wooden table front edge`
- `window frame on the left wall`
- `doorway in the background`

### 6.3 位置描述模板

```text
The main subject is the full-body character standing [anchor-based position], with [key object] positioned [relative relation] and [secondary scene element] visible in the background.
```

示例：

```text
The main subject is the full-body character standing at the center of the multi-ringed circular texture on the stone platform, with the lava flows surrounding the platform edge and the cave opening softly visible in the background.
```

### 6.4 参考图引用必须绑定具体锚点（Reference-to-Anchor Rule）

当某一帧需要控制人物位置时，引用参考图时不要只写 `Use case_frame01.png for the character position`，而应写清楚从参考图中继承哪些可见锚点。

推荐：

```text
Use case_frame01.png for the plaza layout, pillar location, circular cobblestone rings, pillar base shadow, and lighting direction.
```

不推荐：

```text
Use case_frame01.png for the character position and scene.
```

原因：`character position` 太泛，模型可能只继承“大概在场景里”；`pillar location / circular cobblestone rings / pillar base shadow` 才能把位置控制绑定到具体可见物。

### 6.5 具体空间关系规则：不用 distance，用可见落点

控制人物与目标物远近时，禁止只写：

```text
closer to the pillar
farther from the pillar
reduced distance
increased distance
near the object
move two steps forward
```

这些词要么过于抽象，要么会诱导模型表现“走路动作”，而不是改变人物最终位置。

应改成最终画面中的可见关系：

```text
The front boot is on the narrow stone band immediately around the circular pillar base.
```

```text
The boot toe almost touches the outer edge of the pillar base.
```

```text
No complete cobblestone ring is visible between the front boot and the pillar base.
```

```text
The rear boot remains on the first inner cobblestone ring behind him.
```

如果需要人物远离目标物，应写：

```text
Several full cobblestone rings remain visible between his boots and the pillar base.
```

```text
Both boots are outside the outer cracked circular boundary, while the pillar base stays several rings ahead.
```

核心原则：

> 不描述“距离变近/变远”，而描述“脚、手、道具与场景锚点之间具体看得见的相对关系”。

### 6.6 只控制最终位置，不描述移动过程

如果当前帧的目标只是改变人物位置，不要写 `taking two steps forward`、`walking closer`、`moving toward` 这类过程性动作。它们容易让模型生成跨步、奔跑、衣摆飘动等多余动作。

推荐写最终状态：

```text
Place Xiao Yan directly beside the stone pillar, facing the pillar. His front boot is on the narrow stone band immediately around the circular pillar base.
```

不推荐写过程：

```text
Xiao Yan takes two full steps forward toward the pillar.
```

当确实需要表现运动过程时，才写 `stepping / walking / running`，并且要同时说明脚的最终落点。

### 6.7 位置控制与姿态表现分离规则

当目标是控制人物最终位置时，**最终落点必须优先于动作过程**。不要让 `walking / taking two steps / moving closer` 这类动作词承担位置控制功能。

允许出现可接受的单步姿态，例如：

```text
one-step forward stance
one foot forward
a stable forward-stepping stance
```

但这些词只能作为姿势补充，必须同时写清楚鞋子与场景锚点的关系。

推荐写法：

```text
Place Xiao Yan directly beside the stone pillar, facing the pillar. His front boot is on the narrow stone band immediately around the circular pillar base, with the boot toe almost touching the outer edge of the pillar base; his rear boot remains on the first inner cobblestone ring behind him, forming a stable one-step forward stance.
```

不推荐写法：

```text
Xiao Yan walks closer to the pillar.
```

```text
Xiao Yan takes two full steps forward toward the pillar.
```

```text
Emphasize the reduced distance between Xiao Yan and the pillar.
```

核心原则：

> 如果要控制位置，先写“脚落在哪里”；如果要表现姿态，再补充“是否为单步姿态”。跨一步可以接受，但不能用“跨一步”代替位置锚点。

## 7. 朝向关系规则（Subject-Object-Camera Orientation）

写每一帧 prompt 前，必须先判断两个关系：

1. **Subject–Object Relation**：人物面向目标物、背向目标物、侧对目标物，还是正在越过/靠近/触碰目标物？
2. **Subject–Camera Relation**：人物面向镜头、背对镜头、侧对镜头，还是过肩/俯视只出现局部？

这两个关系决定 prompt 应描述哪些身体细节，不能混写。

### 7.1 人物面向镜头时

可以描述：

- facial structure
- eyes / gaze
- facial expression（仅当剧情需要）
- front robe pattern
- collar, chest, belt, sleeves
- hands in front of the body

示例：

```text
The character faces the camera in a medium shot, clearly showing his facial structure, front robe pattern, collar, belt, and both hands forming a spell gesture at chest level.
```

### 7.2 人物背对镜头时

必须避免：

- facial structure
- expression
- calm face
- eyes closed
- focused expression
- handsome face
- looking at the camera

应描述：

- back of hairstyle
- back of head
- shoulder line
- back-view robe folds
- waist belt from behind
- sleeves from behind
- boots and stable stance
- target object in front of him

示例：

```text
The main subject is the full-body character standing with his back facing the camera, looking toward the single stone pillar centered in the frame. Clearly show the back of his hairstyle, shoulder line, back-view robe folds, waist belt, sleeves, boots, and stable standing posture.
```

### 7.3 人物面向目标物但背对镜头

这是最容易失败的情况。必须明确写：

```text
back view of the character
```

```text
his back facing the camera
```

```text
the target object is directly in front of him
```

并加入负向约束：

```text
Do not show the character's face, do not describe facial expression.
```

### 7.4 人物侧对镜头时

应描述侧面可见细节，不要同时写完整正脸和完整背面。

示例：

```text
The character is shown in a three-quarter side view, with one shoulder closer to the camera, the side profile partially visible, and the glowing object in front of his extended hand.
```

### 7.5 过肩镜头

应描述前景肩膀、后脑、发丝，目标物是画面重点。

```text
Camera: over-the-shoulder view from behind the character. Keep the shoulder, back of the head, and part of the hair in the foreground, while the glowing spatial rift in front of him is the main focus.
```

### 7.6 可见/不可见细节规则（Visibility Control）

不要用 `partially visible`、`slightly visible`、`subtle profile`、`a little side face` 这类模糊限制来控制人物露出多少脸。模型更容易抓住其中的强触发词，例如 `side profile`，从而主动展示完整侧脸。

错误示例：

```text
Clearly show his side profile partially visible.
```

推荐改成：

```text
Clearly show the back of his head, high ponytail, rear shoulder line, back-view robe folds, waist belt from behind, sleeve shape, and boots. The eyes, nose, mouth, and side face are not visible because the back of the head, hair, and shoulder block the face from the camera.
```

当人物背对镜头或三分之二背对镜头时，应使用“可见细节 + 不可见结构”双重约束：

- 可见：`back of head`, `high ponytail`, `rear shoulder line`, `back-view robe folds`, `waist belt from behind`, `sleeves from behind`, `boots`；
- 不可见：`eyes`, `nose`, `mouth`, `side face`, `facial expression`。

核心原则：

> 不描述“露一点点侧脸”，而描述“镜头能看到哪些背面细节，以及哪些脸部结构不能出现”。

## 8. Camera Layer 规则

每一帧 prompt 必须包含 camera layer：

```text
Camera: [shot size], [camera angle/viewpoint], [composition focus], [optional motion feeling].
```

推荐稳定镜头词：

```text
wide shot
full-body shot
medium shot
close-up
insert shot
eye-level view
low-angle view
high-angle view
overhead view
over-the-shoulder view
back view
static wide shot
dynamic low-angle shot
```

谨慎使用：

```text
extreme Dutch angle
complex 180-degree orbit
rapid camera roll
abstract POV
```

### 8.1 不要只写镜头术语

镜头术语后必须接可见内容：

```text
Camera: insert shot, overhead view, the fingertips and the glowing crack fill most of the frame.
```

不要只写：

```text
Camera: insert shot.
```

### 8.2 精确数字不是优先项

不优先写：

```text
rotate exactly 37 degrees
```

更推荐写：

```text
change the view to behind the character's right shoulder, with his shoulder and hair in the foreground and the glowing rift directly ahead.
```

## 9. 英文一段式 Prompt 规则

最终可复制 prompt 必须是一个完整英文自然段，不能拆成多段或项目符号。

必须包含：

1. 文件名；
2. 参考哪些真实文件名；
3. 每张图分别参考什么；
4. camera layer；
5. 位置锚点；
6. 朝向关系；
7. 当前动作、视线、道具状态；
8. 必要约束；
9. 风格句。

### 9.1 最小模板

```text
Generate case_frame0x.png: Use [real filename] for [specific reference targets and scene anchors], and use [real filename] for [specific identity/style targets]. Camera: [shot size], [camera angle/viewpoint], [composition focus]. The main subject is [visible subject] positioned at [concrete anchor-based location], with [subject-object relation] and [subject-camera relation]. Clearly show [visible body/prop details appropriate to this viewpoint], [final visible spatial relation such as boot-to-base / hand-to-object / object-to-table-edge], and [object state]. If needed, state which face/body structures are not visible. Keep [identity/lighting/environment] unchanged. Do not [unwanted changes or misleading visible details]. Cinematic Chinese fantasy 3D animation style.
```

### 9.2 正面人物示例

```text
Generate case_frame02.png: Use case_frame01.png for the character position, platform layout, and lava glow state, and use case_char_01.png for the character identity, facial structure, hairstyle, blue robe, belt, boots, and accessories. Camera: medium shot, eye-level view, centered composition focusing on the character from head to knees. The main subject is Wang Lin standing at the center of the multi-ringed circular texture on the stone platform, facing the camera with both hands pressed together at chest level. Clearly show his facial structure, front robe pattern, collar, waist belt, hand gesture, and the warm lava glow reflecting on his sleeves. Keep the platform position, lava environment, character identity, and outfit unchanged. Do not add new characters, weapons, or extra effects. Cinematic Chinese fantasy 3D animation style.
```

### 9.3 背对镜头示例

```text
Generate case_frame01.png: Use case_char_01.png for the character identity, hairstyle, outfit silhouette, back-view clothing details, belt, sleeves, boots, and accessories, and use case_scene_01.png for the scene layout, stone pillar position, cobblestone texture, distant architecture silhouettes, and warm daylight lighting direction. Camera: wide shot, eye-level view, static establishing composition, back view of the character. The main subject is the full-body character standing in the foreground with his back facing the camera, looking toward the single stone pillar centered in the frame. Clearly show the back of his hairstyle, shoulder line, robe folds, waist belt, sleeves, boots, and stable standing posture, while keeping the stone pillar directly in front of him and the distant architecture silhouettes softly visible in the background. Emphasize the spatial relationship between the character's back, the central stone pillar, the plaza ground, and the surrounding scene. Keep the character posture natural and the scene elements unchanged. Do not show the character's face, do not describe facial expression, and do not add new characters, weapons, or environmental effects. Cinematic Chinese fantasy 3D animation style.
```

### 9.4 场景锚点定位示例

```text
Generate case_frame01.png: Use case_char_01.png for the character identity, hairstyle, blue robe with silver embroidery, belt, boots, and accessories, and use case_scene_01.png for the lava cave layout, multi-ringed circular texture on the stone platform, platform edge stones, molten lava flow direction, and dim orange ambient lighting. Camera: wide shot, eye-level view, static establishing composition. The main subject is the full-body character standing at the center of the multi-ringed circular texture on the stone platform, with the platform edge stones evenly surrounding him and the lava flows visible around the platform. Clearly show the spatial relationship between Wang Lin, the ring pattern under his feet, the platform edge, and the surrounding lava cave environment. Keep the character posture natural, lava glow consistent, and environment unchanged. Do not add new characters, props, weapons, or extra effects. Cinematic Chinese fantasy 3D animation style.
```

## 10. 常见镜头模板

### 10.1 Establishing wide shot

```text
Camera: wide shot, eye-level view, static establishing composition. The main subject is the full-body character within the complete environment, positioned using a visible scene anchor, clearly showing the spatial relationship between the character, the key object, and the surrounding scene.
```

### 10.2 Medium action shot

```text
Camera: medium shot, eye-level view, the character from head to knees fills the center of the frame. Clearly show the body movement, hand position, gaze direction, and the nearby key object, while keeping the environment as secondary context.
```

### 10.3 Close-up front view

```text
Camera: close-up, eye-level or low-angle view, the face and upper chest fill most of the frame. Clearly show the facial structure, hairstyle, collar, shoulder details, gaze direction, and necessary expression only if the story requires it.
```

### 10.4 Insert shot

```text
Camera: insert shot, overhead or slightly high-angle view, the hand and key object fill most of the frame. Clearly show the fingertips, knuckles, object surface, contact point, and the glow or texture change around the contact area.
```

### 10.5 Back view

```text
Camera: back view, medium-wide shot. Clearly show the back of the character, back of hairstyle, shoulder line, back-view clothing texture, waist belt, sleeves, boots, and the key object positioned directly in front of the character. Do not show the character's face or facial expression.
```

### 10.6 Over-the-shoulder view

```text
Camera: over-the-shoulder view from behind the character. Keep the shoulder, back of the head, and part of the hair in the foreground, while the key object in front of the character is the main focus.
```

### 10.7 Static aftermath shot

```text
Camera: static wide shot, calm aftermath composition, no character in frame. Clearly show the environment inheriting the changed object state, such as fading glow, remaining particles, displaced props, or an empty platform.
```

## 11. 禁止误导词规则

### 11.1 背面镜头禁止词

当人物背对镜头、过肩镜头、只显示背影或后脑时，不要写：

```text
facial structure
facial expression
calm expression
focused expression
eyes closed
looking at the camera
handsome face
front robe pattern
```

应改写为：

```text
back of hairstyle
back of head
shoulder line
back-view robe folds
waist belt from behind
sleeves from behind
boots
stable stance
```

### 11.2 非强情绪帧避免表情词

如果只是普通过渡帧，不要写痛苦、惊恐、愤怒、微微痛苦、略微紧张等容易被放大的表情词。

推荐：

```text
without emphasizing any exaggerated facial expression
```

### 11.3 抽象词替换表

以下抽象词或模糊限制词应尽量避免。需要表达同类含义时，改成可见锚点、最终状态、身体部位或不可见结构。

| 不推荐 | 问题 | 推荐替换 |
|---|---|---|
| `reduced distance` / `closer` | 抽象距离，模型难判断 | `front boot on the narrow stone band around the pillar base` |
| `farther` / `increased distance` | 抽象距离 | `several full cobblestone rings visible between the boots and the pillar base` |
| `near the pillar` | 位置模糊 | `boot toe almost touching the outer edge of the pillar base` |
| `taking two steps forward` | 容易生成跨步过程或跨步过大 | `already standing beside the pillar; front boot placed at ...` |
| `one-step forward stance` 单独使用 | 姿态词不能控制最终位置 | `front boot on the narrow stone band around the pillar base; rear boot on the first inner ring, forming a stable one-step forward stance` |
| `side profile partially visible` | 会触发完整侧脸 | `eyes, nose, mouth, and side face are not visible; back of head and shoulder block the face` |
| `slightly / subtly / a little` | 限制弱，容易被放大或忽略 | 直接写可见程度：`only the back of the head and shoulder are visible` |
| `dramatic angle` | 过泛 | `low-angle view, camera below waist level, tilted upward` |

## 12. Candidate Selection：多候选图选择机制

当用户一次上传同一帧多张候选图时，模型必须先选择，不得直接给下一帧 prompt。

选择的不是“最好看”的图，而是最适合作为正式分镜帧并承接下一帧的图。

优先级：

1. Prompt Match：是否符合当前 prompt；
2. Anchor Accuracy：人物/道具是否落在正确场景锚点；
3. Orientation Accuracy：人物与目标物、人物与镜头的朝向是否正确；
4. Character Consistency：人物身份、发型、服装是否一致；
5. Scene Continuity：场景、光源、物体位置是否继承；
6. Camera Accuracy：镜头类型、景别、机位是否正确；
7. Story Progression：是否体现当前剧情节点；
8. Future Usability：是否适合作为下一帧参考；
9. Visual Quality：是否有畸形、模糊、错误元素。

### 12.1 候选选择输出模板

```md
推荐选择：case_frame03_B.png，并将其命名为正式的 case_frame03.png。

理由：B 的人物位置最准确，脚下对齐场景中的圆形纹理锚点；人物朝向与镜头关系正确；角色、服装、光源和场景结构稳定。A 的人物偏离锚点，C 的镜头变化不足，D 虽然画面更炫但新增了不需要的特效。下一帧可以基于 case_frame03.png 继续推进。
```

如果全部不合格：

```md
这一组候选图都不建议选为 case_frame03.png：共同问题是人物没有落在指定场景锚点，且人物朝向与目标物关系错误。建议重生成当前帧。

Regenerate case_frame03.png: ...
```

## 13. 逐帧质检规则

质检时检查：

### 13.1 Anchor Accuracy

- 人物是否落在 prompt 指定的场景锚点上？
- 是否只是大概在“中心”，但没有对齐具体纹理、边缘、柱子、桌角等？
- 道具是否放在指定的参考物之间或指定空白区域？

### 13.2 Orientation Accuracy

- 人物是否面向目标物？
- 人物是否按要求面向或背对镜头？
- 背面镜头是否错误露出正脸？
- prompt 中是否错误使用了与镜头不匹配的正面/背面细节？

### 13.3 Camera Accuracy

- 是否符合计划中的 shot type？
- 是否与上一帧有明显镜头变化？
- 是否通过可见内容体现镜头，而不是只在 prompt 里写了术语？

### 13.4 Continuity

- 人物身份、服装、发型是否一致；
- 场景光源、结构、道具状态是否继承；
- 当前动作是否承接上一帧；
- 是否新增无关人物、道具、武器或错误特效。

---

## 14. 重生成判定

出现以下情况，应重生成当前帧：

- 人物没有对齐指定场景锚点；
- 人物与目标物关系错误，例如应面向石柱却面向镜头；
- 人物与镜头关系错误，例如应背对镜头却露出正脸；
- 背面/过肩镜头中出现 facial expression 或正脸细节；
- 镜头类型错误，例如要求 close-up 却仍是 full-body shot；
- 连续两帧镜头过于相似；
- 动作顺序错误或没有推进；
- 新增无关人物、道具、武器或环境特效；
- 场景状态没有继承；
- 所有候选图都不适合作为下一帧参考。

## 16. 核心执行口令

写每一帧 prompt 前，必须先问：

1. What is the story beat of this frame?
2. What camera change happens compared with the previous frame?
3. What real filenames should be referenced, and what specific anchors should be inherited from each file?
4. What scene anchor should locate the character or object?
5. Is the prompt using abstract distance words, or concrete visible relations such as boot-to-base / hand-to-object / object-to-table-edge?
6. What is the final visible position, not the movement process?
7. What is the subject-object relation?
8. What is the subject-camera relation?
9. Should this shot describe front-view details, back-view details, side-view details, or only local details?
10. Which structures should be visible, and which structures must not appear?
11. What should the viewer clearly see in this shot?
12. Which words may mislead the model, such as side profile, facial expression, closer, farther, slightly, or partially visible?
13. Does this frame provide a stable reference for the next frame?

最终输出必须是英文一段式 prompt。

## 17. 一句话总结

v5.2 的核心不是让 prompt 更长，而是让 prompt 更明确：

```text
Use visible anchors to locate the subject, replace abstract relations with concrete visible details, use orientation logic to control what can and cannot be seen, then use camera variation to tell the story.
```
